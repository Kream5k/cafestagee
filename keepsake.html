Behind the Café
Cafestage is a fan-made alternate universe inspired by ALIEN STAGE, reimagining its characters in a cozy café setting. This is an independent, unofficial fan project created purely out of appreciation for the original series. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the original creators.

Special Thanks
To everyone who supported, inspired, and contributed to this project, thank you for helping bring Cafestage to life. And to the original creators and artists behind ALIEN STAGE, thank you for creating a world that continues to inspire so many creative interpretations.
